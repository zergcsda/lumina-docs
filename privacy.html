Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-01-11

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumina handles your information. Lumina does not require account registration and does not operate its own servers for personal data.

Information We Collect

  • No personal data is collected by Lumina on our own servers.
  • Gameplay data such as scores and settings are stored locally on your device.

Apple Services

  • If you sign in to Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center data under Apple's policies.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your data with third parties, except for Apple services you choose to use (Game Center, App Store).

Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear data. Game Center data is managed by Apple.

Children's Privacy

Lumina does not collect personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

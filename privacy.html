Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026 · Applies to Lumina for iPhone and iPad, version 1.0 and later

Data Not Collected

Lumina does not collect any data from this app. This matches the App Privacy details we declare on the App Store.

Account or sign-inNone
Network accessNone. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.
Advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKsNone
Tracking (as defined by Apple)None. The app does not request tracking permission.
In-app purchasesNone. Lumina is a one-time App Store purchase.
Data stored on your deviceScores, best times, level progress, sound/haptics settings and language choice

What stays on your device

Lumina saves your scores, level progress, best times and settings in the app's local storage on your iPhone or iPad. This data never leaves your device through Lumina. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

Purchases

Apple processes the purchase of Lumina through the App Store under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Sharing and sale

We do not collect personal information, so there is nothing to share or sell.

Deleting your data

Deleting Lumina from your device removes all of its local data. If your device backups are turned on, iCloud or computer backups that you control may also contain this data.

Children

Lumina is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.

Emailing us

If you choose to send a suggestion or support request, your email app sends it to us directly. The draft Lumina prepares includes the app version, device type and iOS version, which you can edit or remove before sending. We use what you send, including your email address, only to read and reply to your message, and we do not share it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@keepsub.com

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日 · 适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 版 Lumina 1.0 及以后版本

未收集数据

Lumina 不会通过本 App 收集任何数据，与我们在 App Store「App 隐私」中的声明一致。

账号或登录无
网络访问无。App 完全离线运行，不发起任何网络请求。
广告、统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK无
跟踪（按 Apple 定义）无，不会请求跟踪权限。
App 内购买无。Lumina 在 App Store 一次性买断。
保存在设备上的数据分数、最佳用时、关卡进度、声音与触感设置、语言选择

保存在你设备上的内容

Lumina 把分数、关卡进度、最佳用时和设置保存在 iPhone 或 iPad 的本地存储中。这些数据不会经由 Lumina 离开你的设备，我们无法查看，也无法替你恢复。

购买

Lumina 的购买由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，适用 Apple 的条款与隐私政策。我们不会获得你的姓名、Apple 账户或支付信息。

共享与出售

我们不收集个人信息，因此不存在共享或出售。

删除数据

从设备上删除 Lumina 即会移除其全部本地数据。如果你开启了设备备份，你自己的 iCloud 或电脑备份中也可能包含这些数据。

儿童

Lumina 适合所有年龄，不向任何人（包括儿童）收集个人信息。

变更

如果未来版本改变数据处理方式，我们会在该版本发布前更新本页面和 App Store 隐私信息。

给我们发邮件

如果你选择提交建议或寻求支持，邮件会由你的邮件 App 直接发送给我们。Lumina 预填的草稿包含 App 版本、设备类型和系统版本，发送前你可以修改或删除。我们只用你发来的内容（包括邮箱地址）阅读和回复你的来信，不会共享给任何人。

联系

隐私相关问题请联系：support@keepsub.com
